# Glossary

## Ascend NPU
A neural processing unit developed by Huawei (Ascend 310B4 on Orange Pi AIpro), executing tensor algebra via the CANN runtime and `torch_npu`.

## Settlement
The iterative relaxation process of a neural network towards a stable equilibrium point under sensory drive and recurrent synaptic constraints.

## Equation Residual
The numerical error vector measuring deviation from the steady-state equilibrium condition ($v - (W s + b) = 0$). An action is qualified only when this residual falls below tolerance.

## Qualification
The gating check verifying whether free or nudged equilibrium states meet the defined mathematical convergence tolerance before permitting actions or parameter updates.

## System 1
The reflexive, continuing animal-like equilibrium brain providing perception, associative memory, working trace, and action in a coupled neural graph.

## Routine and Repair
The continuous life cycle where an agent answers greedily while calm, and samples, learns, and repairs representations upon encountering unexpected outcomes or reward deficits.

## EVAL Benchmark
A standardized, repeatable experimental protocol comparing behavioral fidelity, numerical convergence, latency percentiles, and throughput across different hardware backends under matched seeds and task schedules.
